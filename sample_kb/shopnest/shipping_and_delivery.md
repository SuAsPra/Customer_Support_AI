# ShopNest Shipping and Delivery Guide

## Shipping Options
- **Standard Shipping**: 3-5 business days, free on orders above $25.
- **Express Shipping**: 1-2 business days, additional fee shown at checkout.
- **Same-Day Delivery**: available in select cities for eligible items ordered before 12 PM local time.

## Order Processing
- Orders are typically processed within **1 business day**. During sales or holidays, processing may take up to 2 business days.
- You will receive an email/SMS with tracking details once shipped.

## Delayed or Missing Packages
- If your order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date, check tracking for the latest carrier update.
- If tracking shows **delivered** but you haven't received it: check with household members/neighbors, look around the delivery area, and wait 24 hours as carriers sometimes mark early. If still missing, contact support within 48 hours to start an investigation.
- If tracking hasn't moved for **48 hours**, we can contact the carrier and offer options (reshipment or refund) based on investigation results.

## Damaged Packages
- If the package arrives damaged, take photos of the outer box and item. Report within 7 days via Orders > Report Issue > Damaged Item for replacement or refund.

## International Shipping
- International delivery times vary by destination (typically 7-14 business days). Customs duties/taxes may apply and are the customer's responsibility unless stated otherwise.
