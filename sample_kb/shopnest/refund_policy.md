# ShopNest Refund and Return Policy

## Returns Eligibility
- Most items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery if unused, in original packaging, and with tags/receipt.
- **Non-returnable items**: perishable goods, personal care items, gift cards, and final-sale items marked at checkout.
- Damaged or defective items can be reported within **7 days** of delivery for free return or replacement.

## Refund Timelines
- Once a return is received and inspected, refunds are approved within **2 business days**.
- **Credit/debit card**: 5-7 business days to reflect.
- **UPI/net banking**: 3-5 business days.
- **ShopNest Wallet**: within 24 hours after approval.
- **Cash on Delivery orders**: refunded to bank account or wallet after you provide payout details.

## Duplicate or Incorrect Charges
- If you were **charged twice** for the same order, verify in your bank statement whether one entry is a pending authorization. Pending authorizations usually drop off within 2-5 business days.
- If both charges are posted, contact support with order ID, amounts, and transaction dates. After verification, we issue a refund for the duplicate charge within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

## Cancellations
- Orders can be cancelled before shipment for a full refund.
- If already shipped, you may refuse delivery or initiate a return after delivery; refund timing follows the return process.
- Subscription cancellations stop future billing. Current-period charges follow the subscription terms shown at purchase.

## Escalation and Exceptions
- If a refund is delayed beyond stated timelines, request a **refund status review**. Support can escalate to the Payments team with your case ID.
- Policy exceptions (outside return window, missing packaging) require supervisor approval and are handled case-by-case.
