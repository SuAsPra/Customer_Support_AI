# ShopNest Customer Support FAQ

## Account and Login
**Q: I forgot my password. How can I reset it?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your registered email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox. The link expires in 30 minutes. If you don't receive it, check spam or request a new link.

**Q: How do I update my email address or phone number?**
A: Go to Account Settings > Profile. For security, changing your email requires confirmation via a code sent to your old email.

**Q: My account is locked. What should I do?**
A: Accounts may be temporarily locked after multiple failed login attempts. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or use password reset. If you suspect unauthorized access, contact support immediately.

## Orders and Tracking
**Q: Where can I track my order?**
A: Go to Orders > Select your order > Track Package. Tracking updates within 24 hours of shipment. If tracking hasn't updated for 48 hours after the estimated ship date, contact support.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?**
A: Address changes are possible only before the order is packed (usually within 1-2 hours). Go to Orders > Modify, or contact support urgently. Once shipped, we cannot reroute packages.

**Q: I received the wrong item.**
A: We’re sorry. Report it within 7 days via Orders > Report Issue > Wrong Item. We’ll arrange a free return pickup and ship the correct item or issue a refund based on your preference.

## Payments
**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and ShopNest Wallet. Cash on Delivery is available on eligible orders.

**Q: My payment failed but money was deducted.**
A: Failed payments with deducted amounts are often pending authorizations. They typically reverse automatically within 2-5 business days depending on your bank. If not reversed after 5 days, contact support with the transaction reference.
