# Sample Customer Scenarios (ShopNest, fictional)

## Scenario 1 — Duplicate charge (billing, likely to escalate)
- **Product context**: ShopNest e-commerce subscription
- **Scenario**: Customer reports being charged twice this month for the same subscription.
- **Persona (simulator)**: Impatient
- **Emotional progression**: frustrated → angry if no quick resolution → relieved if refund/timeline confirmed
- **Why escalation risk**: money impact, repeated concern, possible supervisor request if delayed.

## Scenario 2 — Delivered but missing package (delivery)
- **Product context**: ShopNest order delivery
- **Scenario**: Tracking shows delivered but customer has not received the package.
- **Persona**: Confused
- **Emotional progression**: worried → anxious → reassured with clear investigation steps

## Scenario 3 — App login issues (technical/account)
- **Product context**: ShopNest mobile app
- **Scenario**: Customer cannot log in and is not receiving password reset emails.
- **Persona**: Patient
- **Emotional progression**: neutral → slightly frustrated → satisfied after troubleshooting

## Scenario 4 — Wrong item received (fulfillment)
- **Product context**: ShopNest order fulfillment
- **Scenario**: Customer received the wrong item and wants a replacement quickly.
- **Persona**: Impatient
- **Emotional progression**: annoyed → calm if replacement arranged promptly

## Scenario 5 — Refund delay beyond timeline (billing, escalation-prone)
- **Product context**: ShopNest returns/refunds
- **Scenario**: Customer returned an item 10 business days ago and has not received the refund to their card.
- **Persona**: Angry
- **Emotional progression**: angry → very angry/escalation demand → de-escalates only with concrete review/escalation action

*All scenarios and data are fictional for project use only.*
